Motor Insurance Renewal:

Purpose: To ensure policies are renewed within the required timeframe and without unnecessary coverage gaps.

Process:

Identify policies approaching their renewal date.

- Review existing policy details, claims history, vehicle information, and coverage requirements.

- Validate any changes to customer or vehicle information.

- Calculate the applicable renewal premium and confirm the coverage terms.

- Process the renewal and provide the updated policy documents to the customer.

Key Control: Renewal details should be validated before payment confirmation and policy activation.
