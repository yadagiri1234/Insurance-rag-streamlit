Motor Insurance Claim Assessment & Settlement:

Purpose: To ensure claims are assessed fairly, accurately, and in accordance with policy terms.

Process:

- Review the claim details, policy coverage, supporting documents, and inspection or survey findings.

- Validate the cause and extent of damage or loss against the applicable policy coverage.

- Identify exclusions, deductibles, discrepancies, or additional information requirements.

- Obtain the required approvals based on the applicable authority limits.

- Approve and settle the claim, or communicate the appropriate reason when the claim cannot be approved.

Key Control: Claim decisions and settlement amounts should be supported by documented assessment and approvals.
