Motor Insurance Claim Registration:

Purpose: To establish a consistent process for registering and initiating motor insurance claims.

Process:

- Receive the claim notification from the policyholder or authorized representative.

- Verify the policy status and confirm that the reported incident falls within the policy period.

- Capture accident, vehicle, driver, location, and incident details.

- Collect required documents, photographs, police reports, or other supporting evidence where applicable.

- Register the claim and provide the claimant with the claim reference and next steps.

Key Control: Claims should be registered with complete and accurate incident information.
