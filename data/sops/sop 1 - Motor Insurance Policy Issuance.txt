Motor Insurance Policy Issuance

Purpose: To ensure motor insurance policies are issued accurately and consistently.

Process:

- Collect customer, vehicle, and required supporting documents.

- Verify vehicle details, ownership, registration, usage, and previous insurance information.

- Determine applicable coverage, premium, deductibles, and policy terms.

- Complete required validations and approvals before policy issuance.

- Issue the policy and communicate the coverage details and key conditions to the customer.


Key Control: No policy should be issued until all mandatory information and validations are completed.
