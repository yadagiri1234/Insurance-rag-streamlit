Policy & Claim Record Management:

Purpose: To ensure accurate maintenance and closure of motor insurance records.

Process:

- Maintain complete records of policies, endorsements, claims, approvals, payments, and customer communications.

- Update policy and claim systems promptly following each completed transaction or process stage.

- Verify that all required documents and approvals are available before closure.

- Maintain records according to applicable retention and compliance requirements.

- Close the policy or claim only after confirming that all outstanding activities have been completed.

Key Control: Records should be complete, accurate, traceable, and readily available for audit or review.
