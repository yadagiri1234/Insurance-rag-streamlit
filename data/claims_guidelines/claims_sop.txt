Claims Processing Guidelines

1. Claim Submission
The claim should be submitted with all required information and supporting documents. Ensure that the details provided are accurate and complete. Claims should be submitted within the applicable timelines. Incomplete submissions may result in delays or requests for additional information.

2. Eligibility Check
The claim is reviewed to confirm that the claimant is eligible for the requested benefits. Policy details, coverage, and applicable terms are verified. The claim period and relevant conditions are also checked. Any eligibility concerns should be identified before further processing.

3. Document Review
All submitted documents are reviewed for completeness, accuracy, and consistency. Supporting documents should meet the required guidelines and standards. Missing or unclear information may require clarification from the claimant or relevant party. Proper document validation helps ensure accurate claim assessment.

4. Claim Assessment
The claim is evaluated based on the applicable policy terms, guidelines, and supporting evidence. The reviewer determines whether the claim meets the required criteria. Any discrepancies or exceptions are investigated as needed. The assessment should be objective, consistent, and properly documented.

5. Approval & Settlement
Once the assessment is completed, the claim is approved, rejected, or placed on hold for additional information. Approved claims are processed for payment according to the applicable terms. Rejected claims should include the appropriate reason and supporting explanation. All decisions should be communicated clearly and recorded properly.

6. Closure & Recordkeeping
After the claim is resolved, all relevant information and decisions should be recorded. The claim file should contain the required documents and approval details. Records should be maintained according to applicable policies and retention requirements. The claim can then be formally closed once all processing activities are completed.
