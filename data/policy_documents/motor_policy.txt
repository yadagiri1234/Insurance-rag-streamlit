Motor Insurance Policy Guidelines
1. Policy Issuance
The policy should be issued after verifying the vehicle, owner, and required policy details. Ensure that the vehicle information, coverage type, policy period, and insured details are accurate. The applicable premium and coverage terms should be clearly communicated to the policyholder. Any required documents should be collected and validated before policy issuance.

2. Coverage & Eligibility
The policy should clearly define the coverage provided for the insured vehicle. Eligibility is determined based on factors such as vehicle type, usage, policy terms, and applicable regulations. Coverage may include own-damage, third-party liability, theft, accidents, and other specified risks. Policyholders should be informed about exclusions, limitations, deductibles, and applicable conditions.

3. Policy Administration
Policy details should be maintained accurately throughout the policy lifecycle. Changes such as ownership transfer, address updates, vehicle modifications, or changes in usage should be reviewed and recorded appropriately. Renewals should be initiated within the applicable timelines to avoid gaps in coverage. Any policy amendments should be documented and communicated to the policyholder.

4. Accident & Claim Reporting
The policyholder should report an accident, theft, or other covered incident as soon as reasonably possible. Relevant information such as the incident details, vehicle damage, photographs, and supporting documents should be collected. Claims should be assessed against the policy coverage, terms, and applicable conditions. Additional verification or inspection may be required before the claim is processed.

5. Claim Settlement
The claim should be evaluated based on the policy terms, supporting evidence, and assessment of the vehicle damage or loss. Approved claims should be settled according to the applicable coverage and policy conditions. Claims that do not meet the required criteria should be declined with appropriate reasons. All claim decisions, payments, and supporting records should be properly documented.

6. Policy Closure & Recordkeeping
Once the policy or claim process is completed, all relevant records should be updated and maintained. Documents related to the policy, inspections, claims, payments, and communications should be retained according to applicable requirements. The policyholder should receive appropriate confirmation of the final status. Accurate recordkeeping supports compliance, future servicing, and efficient policy management.

7. Execution & Process Steps
- Customer & Vehicle Details: Collect and validate all required customer and vehicle information.

- Policy Assessment: Review eligibility, coverage requirements, premium, and applicable policy terms.

- Policy Processing: Process policy issuance, renewals, endorsements, and other policy changes as required.

- Claims Processing: Register, assess, and process claims in accordance with defined guidelines and approval procedures.

- Validation & Approval: Complete necessary checks, approvals, and documentation at each stage of the process.

- Closure & Monitoring: Confirm final outcomes, update records, and monitor the process through completion.
